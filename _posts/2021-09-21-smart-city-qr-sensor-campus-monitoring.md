I developed a solar-powered smart-city sensor that measured vibration, noise, and odor and visualized the data through a QR code.

I installed one sensor on a utility pole at the Chung-Ang University campus and conducted short-term monitoring.

The deployment supported the **Citizen-Participatory Smart City System Using QR Code Visualization of Sensor Data**, which was developed for the LH Land Development Technology Competition.
