I contributed the essay **"How Successful Research Experiences Lead Undergraduates to Graduate School"** to *Bridge and Structural Engineering*.

The essay reflects on my early undergraduate research, the setbacks I experienced while learning unfamiliar tools, and the 2019 Smart Earthquake Response Idea Contest that gave me my first major research success. It also describes how those experiences shaped my decision to pursue graduate research in infrastructure monitoring.

**Publication:** *Bridge and Structural Engineering*, pp. 56-58.
