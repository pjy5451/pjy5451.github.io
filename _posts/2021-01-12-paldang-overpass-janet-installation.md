I installed JANET at Paldang Overpass in Namyangju, Gyeonggi-do, and collected comparison measurements with a laser Doppler vibrometer (LDV) on January 12, 2021.

The solar-powered setup enabled long-term operation without battery replacement for the first time in this work.
