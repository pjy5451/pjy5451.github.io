At the 12th LH Land Development Technology Competition, our team received an Excellence Award for a citizen-participatory smart city system based on QR code visualization of sensor data. The proposed system measured urban conditions such as tilt, temperature, humidity, odor, and noise, and allowed citizens to submit QR code data through a KakaoTalk channel.

I also led a team in the Construction IoT, AI, and Sensing category of Smart Construction Challenge 2021. We received the Innovation Award, presented as the Korea National Railway Chairman's Award, for an IoT sensor and cloud-based system for continuous bridge displacement monitoring. The system was demonstrated on Paldang Overpass in Gyeonggi Province and Cheongdam 1 Bridge in Seoul.
