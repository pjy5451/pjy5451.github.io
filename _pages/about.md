# Junyoung Park

<p class="intro intro--lead"><strong>I develop <span class="text-highlight text-highlight--system">field sensing systems</span> for data-driven infrastructure.</strong></p>

<p>I am a structural monitoring researcher who enjoys taking sensing systems <strong class="text-highlight text-highlight--system">beyond the laboratory</strong> and into real bridges, roads, vehicles, and prefabricated structures. Through <strong class="text-highlight text-highlight--metric">10+ field tests</strong>, I have worked across sensor and DAQ development, field installation, edge-to-cloud data pipelines, signal processing, and engineering interpretation.</p>

<p>What interests me most is not sensing technology by itself, but whether a system can remain reliable under real operating conditions and produce evidence that engineers can actually use.</p>

<p>At UDNS, I currently develop and validate <strong class="text-highlight text-highlight--system">WIM (Weigh-in-Motion)</strong> and <strong class="text-highlight text-highlight--system">OBM (On-Board Mass)</strong> systems for freight transportation safety. My previous research focused on IoT-based bridge monitoring, displacement estimation, and scalable cloud-connected sensing systems.</p>
